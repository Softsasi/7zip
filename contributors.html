The Person Behind 7-Zip

👨‍💻
Igor Pavlov
Creator & Lead Developer

Igor Pavlov created 7-Zip and the LZMA algorithm. He has been developing and maintaining 7-Zip since 1999, making it one of the most enduring and respected open-source projects in the compression space.
