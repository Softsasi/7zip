Why 7-Zip?

Best Compression Ratio

Our native 7z format with LZMA/LZMA2 provides a compression ratio up to 30-70% better than standard zip.

AES-256 Encryption

Military-grade encryption for both files and filenames. Secure your data with confidence.

Universal Support

Unpack ARJ, CAB, CHM, CPIO, CramFS, DEB, DMG, FAT, HFS, ISO, LZH, LZMA, MBR, MSI, NSIS, NTFS, RAR, RPM, SquashFS, UDF, VHD, WIM, XAR and Z.

Powerful CLI

A powerful command line version that easily integrates with your scripts and CI/CD pipelines.
